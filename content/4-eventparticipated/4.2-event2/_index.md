# Competition Participation Report: “FCAJ BUILDRATHON 2026 — Season 01”

### Program & Competition Overview
*   **Competition Title**: FCAJ BUILDRATHON 2026 — Season 01
*   **Organizer**: First Cloud Journey (FCAJ) Community in collaboration with AWS Vietnam
*   **Kick-off & Registration Period**: September 2026 (Registration Deadline: September 15, 2026 — 6-Month Competition Journey)
*   **Venues & Grand Finale**: AWS Vietnam Office – 7th Floor, Grand Terra Tower, 36 Cat Linh, Dong Da, Hanoi
*   **Format**: Team Competition (2 – 5 members)
*   **Student Role**: Contestant / Team Member (Cloud & AI Solution Development)
*   **Registered Capstone Topic**: *First Cloud AI Journey — Serverless & Event-Driven Game Backend on AWS*

---

### Philosophy & Mission of Buildrathon
*   **Beyond a Traditional Short Hackathon**: Rather than an ephemeral 48-hour hackathon where prototypes are often abandoned, **FCAJ Buildrathon** is an intensive 6-month engineering journey challenging participants to genuinely **BUILD** an end-to-end production Cloud / AI product.
*   **Core Guiding Tenet**: *"Learn by Building, not just Learn for Knowing"*. The focus is delivering tangible, enterprise-grade software that establishes a lasting technical trajectory for each engineer.
*   **Immersive Work Environment**: Teams have direct access to AWS Vietnam Offices to collaborate, study, and receive 1-on-1 architecture mentorship from AWS Solutions Architects.

*   Attended the official program kick-off and registered the project team at the AWS Hanoi Office prior to the September 15, 2026 deadline.

#### 2. Phase 2 (Post-Internship Engineering Extension):

### Competition Stages & Awards

*   **Grand Finale**: **Top 20 Teams** passing quality verification advance to the final pitching showcase.
*   **Awards & Recognition**: **Top 10 Teams** with highest cumulative scores (evaluating technical execution and attendance diligence) receive official credentials awarded directly at the **AWS Vietnam Office**.
*   **Evaluation Committee**: Composed of AWS specialists alongside external **Industry Experts**, evaluating projects objectively based on technical merit and real-world viability.

---

### Participant Benefits

1.  **Curated AWS Learning Pathways**: Access to curated curricula and hands-on lab resources.
2.  **Dedicated Mentorship**: Direct guidance from AWS Community Heroes, Builders, and Senior Cloud Architects.
3.  **Institutional Endorsement**: Formal corporate internship validation and institutional verification for qualifying deliverables.
4.  **Speaker Opportunities**: Priority invitation to present technical topics at regular community meetups.
5.  **Long-Term Career Network**: Ongoing alumni network supporting career placement and industry partnerships.

### Reflections & Key Takeaways

*   **Proactive Mindset**: Enrolling in the competition during the internship period allowed bridging theoretical cloud concepts into a production-grade software asset.
*   **Team Dynamics & Project Governance**: Learned vital team communication, agile sprint pacing, and collaborative architecture design.
*   **Professional Exposure**: Working directly out of the AWS Hanoi Office instilled confidence and reinforced my dedication to pursuing a career in Cloud, DevOps, and AI Engineering.
