# 5.1. Competition & Team Information

### 1. General Competition Overview

* **Competition Name**: FCAJ BUILDRATHON 2026 — Season 01
* **Organizers**: **First Cloud Journey (FCAJ)** community in collaboration with **Amazon Web Services (AWS) Vietnam**
* **Kick-off & Registration**: September 2026 (Registration Deadline: September 15, 2026 — 6-Month Roadmap)
* **Venue & Grand Finale**: AWS Vietnam Office – 7th Floor, Grand Terra Tower, 36 Cat Linh, Dong Da, Hanoi
* **Format**: Team-based competition (2 – 5 members)
* **Core Philosophy**: *"Build to Learn"*. Unlike conventional 2–3 day hackathons, Buildrathon is an intensive 6-month journey where teams design and deploy a complete, production-grade Cloud & AI solution with direct 1-on-1 mentorship from AWS Solutions Architects.
